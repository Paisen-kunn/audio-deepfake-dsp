# Bundled demo audio attribution

This application includes 24 audio samples (12 labeled real and 12 labeled synthetic)
from **Deepfake Audio Detection Dataset v4** by Gary Stafford:

- Dataset: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/garystafford/deepfake-audio-detection>
- Dataset card / provenance: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/garystafford/deepfake-audio-detection/blob/main/README.md>
- Dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- Upstream dataset revision used for the sample selection:
  `fcf5344bb7f82b54b6b932291326d29750ef1e82`

The WAV files are PCM conversions of selected upstream FLAC samples. The upstream
row IDs are preserved in the WAV filenames. The dataset card describes
constituent audio sources, transformations, and limitations; review it and its terms
before reusing or redistributing the audio outside this application.

Suggested citation:

> Stafford, Gary. *Deepfake Audio Detection Dataset v4*. Hugging Face Datasets,
> https://huggingface.co/datasets/garystafford/deepfake-audio-detection.
